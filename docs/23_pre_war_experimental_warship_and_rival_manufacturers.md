# 23. Pre-War Experimental Warship, Flagship Upgrades & Rival Corporate Manufacturers

## 1. Executive Lore & Scavenger Origin Story

### 1.1 The Scrapline Salvager
Before founding the PMC, the player is not an oligarch, general, or highborn heir. The player begins as a nameless **orbital scrapper** operating a battered tugboat in the **Junk Belt (The Scrapline)**—a lethal graveyard of irradiated starship hulls, shattered satellites, and forgotten orbital debris left behind after the First Solar Clashes.

Life in the Scrapline is brutal: evading syndicate pirate boarding crews, sifting through frozen vacuum debris for scrap metal, and trading low-grade titanium for oxygen cylinders at extortionate depot prices.

### 1.2 The Discovery of Project 'Hephaestus'
While investigating an anomalous gravitational sensor glitch inside a hollowed-out asteroid in the Asteroid Belt fringe, the player's salvager crew discovers a colossal, buried pre-war hull:

> **Vessel Classification:** *USN-X Hephaestus* (Pre-War Mobile Suit Foundry Dreadnought)  
> **Status:** Derelict / Cryo-Quarantine Active / Archeotech Nanite Reactor Dormant  
> **Historical Origin:** Developed in extreme secrecy by an extinct pre-war United Nations Aerospace Directorate prior to the Great Secession. Engineered not merely as a warship, but as a self-sustaining **Deep-Space Automated Mobile Suit Factory Ship**.

At the core of the ship lies the **Molecular Nanite Forging Matrix (The Foundry Core)**—a lost-technology manufacturing engine capable of molecular-level alloy synthesis, rapid frame printing, and modular component assembly from raw planetary ores.

With this ancient marvel revived, the player transforms overnight from a penniless scavenger into the sole proprietor of a mobile, self-sufficient weapons foundry. The player's PMC is born.

## 2. The Warship as a Core Gameplay Engine

The *Hephaestus* is the player's mobile base of operations, travelling across the Sol System to establish forward operating theaters.

### 2.1 Orbital Navigation Mechanics
The warship navigates between the **6 Solar Spheres**:
1. **Earth Sphere** (Low Earth Orbit, Lunar Lagrange Hubs)
2. **Martian Sphere** (Phobos Garrison, Olympus Orbital Perimeter)
3. **The Asteroid Belt** (Ceres Port, Psyche Metal Fringe)
4. **Jovian Outer Sphere** (Europa Hydrothermal Station, Io Staging Grounds)
5. **Saturnian Outer Sphere** (Titan Extraction Sills, Enceladus Station)
6. **Deep Solar Frontier** (Kupier Void, Derelict Outposts)

#### Stationing Advantages:
When the *Hephaestus* is stationed in a specific planetary sphere:
* **Mining Extraction Surge**: All active mining nodes in that sphere receive a **$+50\%$ yield multiplier** due to the ship's localized cargo tenders and automated refining sills.
* **Rapid Tactical Scramble**: Tactical escort sorties respond immediately with **$+15\%$ combat stat boost** from shipboard telemetry links.
* **Orbital Fire Support**: In tactical battles (`rm_combat`) within that sphere, the player can order the warship to unleash **Orbital Kinetic Bombardment** directly onto the combat grid!

### 3.1 The 4 Rival Defense Contractors

#### 1. Valkyrie Heavy Industries (VHI)
* **Corporate Alignment:** The Terran Coalition (State State-Contractor).
* **Headquarters:** Genevan Orbital Ring, Earth.
* **CEO / Magnate:** Baroness Victoria Sterling.
* **Design Philosophy:** Super-heavy ballistic plating, tungsten kinetic cannons, unstoppable trench-line crawlers (*GHA-07 Ironclad*, *BHM-01 Behemoth*).
* **Hostility Trigger:** Replacing Terran mass-production doctrines with player mechs costs them billions in state contracts, prompting corporate litigation and private army strikes.

#### 2. Astraea Orbital Dynamics (AOD)
* **Corporate Alignment:** The Outer Colonies (Spacenoid Aerospace Monopoly).
* **Headquarters:** Galileo Aerospace Complex, Ganymede (Jupiter).
* **Chief Technology Officer:** Dr. Sorel Vance.
* **Design Philosophy:** Micro-gravity vernier agility, composite energy shielding, experimental plasma sabers (*AET-01 Aether*, *HLS-08 Helios*).
* **Hostility Trigger:** Out-competing them in energy core R&D causes them to dispatch high-speed interceptor wings to intercept player ore shipments.

#### 3. Ironclad Scavenger Cartel & Rustworks (RUSTWORKS)
* **Corporate Alignment:** Rogue Mercenary Syndicates & Black Markets.
* **Headquarters:** Scrap Forge Asteroid 'Rust-Pike', Belt.
* **Warlord Industrialist:** 'Grease' Garvin.
* **Design Philosophy:** Cobbled salvage frames, overpowered scavenged diesel-fusion cores, heavy rotary vulcans (*SCR-12 Hyena*, *SLG-04 Slag Reaver*).
* **Hostility Trigger:** Threatening their monopoly on illicit scrap metal and cheap mercenary arms leads to ambush raids on player mining outposts.

#### 4. Hyperion Advanced Tactical Systems (HATS)
* **Corporate Alignment:** Apex Bio-Cybernetics Zaibatsu & Private Defense Investors.
* **Headquarters:** Neo-Tokyo Sub-Orbital Platform, Mars.
* **Managing Director:** Director Renzo Takahashi.
* **Design Philosophy:** Cybernetic pilot neural interfaces, predictive combat AI algorithms, active camouflage stealth cloaks (*ZAI-03 Muramasa*, *SUS-00 Susanoo-X*).
* **Hostility Trigger:** Patent theft accusations and deploying corporate black-ops kill-teams to seize your pre-war foundry core.

## 4. Mechanics: Corporate Competition & Sabotage

1. **Procurement Trial Bidding (Fly-Offs)**:
   * When a superpower issues a procurement contract, rival corps submit competing bids. The player's chassis is scored against rival corporate entries in Speed, Armor, Firepower, and Production Cost.
2. **Corporate Market Share Gauge**:
   * Sol System mobile suit market share starts divided among the 4 rivals. Every time the player licenses a mass-production chassis or completes high-tier procurement contracts, the player's market share expands, directly siphoning revenue from rivals!
3. **Corporate Espionage & Kill-Teams**:
   * As rival market share drops, their hostility meter increases ($0$ to $100$).
   * At $\ge 75$ hostility, rival corporations sponsor mercenary kill-teams and saboteurs to raid your mining rigs or ambush your shipments.

## 5. Technical Directives for GameMaker Implementation

1. **`obj_GameManager` Additions**:
   * `flagship`: Struct tracking name, class, location (`current_sphere`), travel timers, and upgrade tier struct (`production_tier`, `mining_tier`, `defense_tier`, `weapons_tier`).
   * `travel_to_sphere(_target_sphere)`: Commences flagship orbital transit (1–3 turns).
   * `upgrade_flagship(_track_id)`: Increments upgrade tier and deducts Credits/alloys.
   * `rival_corporations`: Struct tracking the 4 rival arms manufacturers, market shares, hostility ratings, and flagship products.
2. **`obj_HQTerminal` Additions**:
   * Dedicated **Flagship Command & Orbital Bridge Console** with visual warship schematic, upgrade track cards, and celestial navigation map.
3. **`obj_BattleManager` Additions**:
   * If `flagship.current_sphere` matches the combat zone, unlocks **[CALL ORBITAL BOMBARDMENT]** action card during player phase!
