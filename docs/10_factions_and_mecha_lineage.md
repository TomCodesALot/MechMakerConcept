## Geopolitical Factions, Dramatis Personae & Comprehensive Mecha Lineage Bible (Y205 - Y215)

### Creative Direction & Influences
*   **Tone:** Grounded military sci-fi, geopolitical realpolitik, corporate espionage, and human tragedy within mechanized armor.
*   **Influences:** The gritty industrial logic of *Front Mission* (Wanzer modularity, corporate syndicates, Huffman Island border tensions), the grounded real-robot weight of classic *Universal Century Gundam* (08th MS Team, 0083 Stardust Memory, Yasuhiko mechanical logic), and the corporate merc warfare of *Armored Core* (competing hyper-corps, pilot cybernetics).

## 1. The Four Major Factions & Strategic Doctrines

### Faction 1: The Terran Coalition (TC) — "Slab, Steam, and Steel"
*   **Alignment:** United Earth Defense Assembly (Earth & Lunar Orbital Hegemony)
*   **Color Liveries:** Military Olive Drab, Cast Gunmetal, Industrial Hazard Yellow
*   **Military Doctrine & Philosophy:**
    *   **Attritional Trench Dominance:** Terran doctrine assumes planetary war is won by logistics, mass, and kinetic pulverization. The Coalition deploys heavy bipedal frames engineered to absorb sustained bombardments and deliver high-caliber solid-slug kinetic shells (105mm–203mm).
    *   **Atmospheric Drop Capability:** Multi-chamber hydraulic shock absorption pistons allow Terran breach lances to execute direct orbital insertion drops without landing craft, crashing through enemy orbital defenses and slamming into bedrock.
    *   **Bureaucratic Standardization:** Vanguard Defense Systems designs every mobile suit so that automotive plants can manufacture interchangeable left and right limb assemblies using universal torque wrenches.
*   **Major Characters:**
    *   **General Deborah Vance (Supreme Director of Planetary Ordnance):** Lunar trench pacification veteran; cold logician treating war as a ledger of metal and meat.
        > *"A mobile suit is not a weapon of glory, Commander. It is a four-million-credit piece of hydraulic machinery designed to outlast the infantryman inside it. Keep the assembly line moving."*
    *   **High Commissioner Arthur Sterling (Senate Defense Liaison):** Political hawk demanding mineral tributes from the colonies while contracting PMCs.
*   **Recruitable Pilots:**
    *   **Lt. Marcus 'Breaker' Cole:** Disillusioned heavy assault ace; cashiered after refusing orders to shell civilian hydroponics. (Role: Linebreaker | Trait: +20% Kinetic DMG).
    *   **Ensign Elena Rostova:** Retinal-implant cybernetic marksman who deserted after discovering corporate falsifications. (Role: Sniper | Trait: +15% Crit Chance).

---

### Faction 2: The Outer Colony League (OCL) — "Vector, Velocity, and Voltage"
*   **Alignment:** Jovian-Larange Liberation Compact (Orbital Communes & Asteroid Mining Habitats)
*   **Color Liveries:** Pure Ceramic White, Ion-Plated Teal, Solar Gold Accents
*   **Military Doctrine & Philosophy:**
    *   **Zero-G 3D Dominance:** The League rejects heavy armor in favor of extreme thrust-to-weight ratios, swept-wing vernier binders, and vector attitude control, allowing colonial interceptors to dance outside Terran firing arcs.
    *   **Directed Energy Supremacy:** Utilizing high-output Helium-3 magnetic confinement fusion reactors, Colony suits prioritize high-compression particle beam rifles, continuous-wave beam snipers, and rapid-draw beam sabers.
    *   **Decentralized Shipyard Stamping:** Modular 3D electron-beam sintered ceramics and titanium-aluminum honeycomb monocoques fabricated inside orbital habitat shipyards.
*   **Major Characters:**
    *   **Fleet Commander Cassian Vane (Supreme Marshall of the Flotilla):** Former cargo pilot risen to supreme insurgent leader.
        > *"Earth treats our people as indentured ore-miners while breathing the oxygen our solar scrubbers produce. Every beam rifle we forge is an invoice for two centuries of colonial tribute."*
    *   **Minister Lyra Thorne (Chief Diplomat & Syndicate Chair):** Pragmatic diplomat managing barter networks.
*   **Recruitable Pilots:**
    *   **Sorren 'Ghost' Lin:** Legendary Jovian interceptor ace slipping through radar nets without engine flare. (Role: Interceptor | Trait: +25% Evasion on Boost).
    *   **Kira Dahl:** Prodigal sensor engineer deploying holographic radar phantom arrays. (Role: EW Specialist | Trait: -20% Enemy Accuracy).

---

### Faction 3: The Rim-Syndicate Cartel (RSC) — "Scrap, Sever, and Snatch"
*   **Alignment:** Scavenger-Industrial Scrap Guild (Asteroid Belt Hollows & Lawless Depots)
*   **Color Liveries:** Rust Iron, Corroded Bronze, Spiked Hazard Crimson
*   **Military Doctrine & Philosophy:**
    *   **Asymmetric Boarding Violence:** Operating out of hollowed-out asteroids, the Cartel specializes in ambush grapples, boarding harpoons, 3,500°C thermite hull lances, and point-blank flak scatter-cannons.
    *   **Live-Combat Cannibalization:** Built with universal clamping collars, Syndicate frames can weld severed enemy hydraulic limbs or thrusters directly into their chassis mid-battle without software recalibration.
    *   **Unshielded Overclocks:** Pirate mechanics routinely bypass safety limiters and inject volatile chemical nitrous into salvaged freight reactors for suicidal speed bursts.
*   **Major Characters:**
    *   **Madame Rosalind Cruz (Matriarch of Scrap Depot 9):** Asteroid Belt scrap baroness controlling black-market military blueprints.
        > *"Earth and the Colonies have been burning each other's fleets for ten years, darling. And every time a million-dollar cockpit pops in vacuum, my boys are there with blowtorches. War is the only reliable commodity."*
    *   **'Iron-Eye' Torval (Reaver Fleet Commodore):** Cybernetic pirate warlord infamous for ambushing mineral transports.
*   **Recruitable Pilots:**
    *   **Nadia 'Spark' Ortiz:** Teenage prodigy mechanic who hotwires pneumatic servos 40% faster under live mortar fire. (Role: Combat Engineer | Trait: +25% Scrap).
    *   **'Jackal' Jax Vance:** Boarding specialist pinning enemy mechs with magnetic harpoons. (Role: Ambush Harpooner | Trait: Pins Enemy Mechs).

---

### Faction 4: Apex Bio-Cybernetics Zaibatsu (ABC) — "Synapse, Silence, and Surgery"
*   **Alignment:** Transhumanist Hyper-Corp Conglomerate (Private Orbital Citadels)
*   **Color Liveries:** Obsidian Gloss, High-Tech Chrome, Neon Vermilion Optic Glow
*   **Military Doctrine & Philosophy:**
    *   **Biomechanical Myomer Musculature:** Replaces clunky hydraulics with synthetic carbon-nanotube artificial muscle bundles that contract with organic fluidity, zero acoustic signature, and eerie agility.
    *   **Direct-Spinal Neural Interface:** Bypasses ocular lag by wiring the pilot's spinal cord directly into the machine's motor servos, cutting weapon discharge latency down to 12 milliseconds.
    *   **Surgical Precision & Infiltration:** Faceted obsidian stealth geometry, ferrite radar-absorbing tiles, cold-gas thrusters, monomolecular vibro-blades, and autonomous AI sub-drones.
*   **Major Characters:**
    *   **Director Renzo Takahashi (Executive VP of Special Weapons):** Cold transhumanist who replaced his emotional limbic system with cognitive processors.
        > *"Human reaction time is limited to two hundred milliseconds. Our neural-interface bypasses ocular processing entirely, cutting weapon discharge latency to twelve. Progress requires shedding biological sentiment."*
    *   **Dr. Vivienne Chen (Chief Neural Architect):** Mastermind behind 'Project Susanoo' spinal neural jacks.
*   **Recruitable Pilots:**
    *   **Null-07 (Ren):** Escaped test subject reacting to projectile trajectories before muzzle flashes. (Role: Neural Duelist | Trait: +2 Actions in Turn 1).
    *   **Maya 'Kestrel' Zhao:** Former flight commander wielding 180° vector collar thrusters. (Role: Drone Carrier | Trait: +1 Autonomous Drone).
