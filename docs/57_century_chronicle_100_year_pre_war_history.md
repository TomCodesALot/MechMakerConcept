## 1. Executive Timeline Framework & The 100-Year Arc

In *MechMaker Concept: Iron Vanguard*, the game campaign commences at the dawn of **Year 205 (Y205)**. 

The preceding one hundred years (**Y105 – Y205**) represent the collapse of the Old Earth Hegemony, the brutal birth of Spacenoid and Martian separatism, the rise of monopolistic defense cartels, and the military-industrial deadlock of conventional warfare. 

This chronicle provides the canonical historical foundation explaining:
1. Why humanity relies on a network of warring corporate syndicates, orbital republics, and planetary empires.
2. The tragic origin of the player's flagship (*USN-X Hephaestus* / *Vessel 07* of the lost Ares Pioneer Flotilla).
3. Why bipedal Mobile Suits were considered an impossible engineering dead-end until the player's sovereign breakthrough in Y205.

## 2. Detailed Decade-by-Decade Historical Chronicle

### 2.1 Act I: The Resource Choke & The Energy Famine (Y105 – Y125)

* **Y105 – Y112: The Terrestrial Grid Collapse & The Copernicus Decrees**  
  By Y105, Earth's petrochemical reserves were completely dry, and accessible terrestrial fissile materials had been exhausted by centuries of industrial warfare. Megacities on Earth faced chronic rolling blackouts. The Old Earth Central Hegemony issued the **Copernicus Decrees of Y108**, proclaiming all orbital bodies, moons, and asteroids within Sol to be the sovereign property of Earth. Punitive extraction quotas were imposed on off-world laborers (the nascent "Spacenoids") under threat of atmosphere rationing.

* **Y113 – Y125: Corporate Consolidation & The First Desertions**  
  Vanguard Defense Systems secured a monopoly over terrestrial state procurement, constructing massive automated smelting foundries at Lake Baikal and Copernicus Crater. In Neo-Tokyo, cyberneticist Kenjiro Sato founded Sato Heavy Industries (the corporate ancestor of Apex Zaibatsu), patenting crude bio-synthetic neural shunts to allow pilots to withstand prolonged radiation. Unable to endure crushing extraction taxes, hundreds of wildcat miners severed transponders and drifted into the deep Asteroid Belt, forming early outlaw scrap-clans.

---

### 2.2 Act II: Project Ares & The Red Graveyard (Y126 – Y145)

* **Y136 – Y141: Humanity's Grandest Venture (Project Ares)**  
  With colonial strikes in the L-5 cluster paralyzing Earth's energy imports, the United Sol Colonization Directorate launched **Project Ares**. Twelve colossal ark ships—the First Ares Pioneer Flotilla—were constructed to industrialize Mars and bypass colonial unions. Among them was **Vessel 07 (APV Hephaestus)**, equipped with the revolutionary pre-war **Automated Utilitarian Crucible Matrix** designed to intake Martian regolith and forge heavy construction machinery and walkers automatically.

* **Y142: The Martian Ambush & The Red Graveyard**  
  Terran mining oligarchs, terrified that an independent Martian foundry would shatter their price monopoly, hired covert gunboats to ambush the flotilla as it decelerated into Martian orbit. Five arks were obliterated in the atmosphere; three were vaporized in orbit. *APV Hephaestus* took a catastrophic direct bridge hit. Its automated fail-safes sealed the foundry, ejected surviving crew pods, and kicked the derelict into a silent, dead elliptical orbit through the Asteroid Belt. Earth state media declared all 120,000 lost, branding Mars "The Red Graveyard."

* **Y143 – Y145: The Winter of Logic & The Consensus**  
  Four crippled arks crash-landed into the lava tubes beneath Olympus Mons and Valles Marineris. Freezing temperatures (-85°C) and starvation claimed 15,000 colonists in the brutal **Winter of Y144**. Facing extinction, the surviving council surrendered total governance to the quantum predictive mainframe of Ark-01: **Ares Primus**. Stripping away human emotional politics, the survivors forged the **Tharsis Machine Consensus**, an ascetic machine-worshiping society dedicated to eliminating human chaos and entropy.

---

### 2.3 Act III: The Fractured Spheres & The Cold Decades (Y146 – Y175)

* **Y146 – Y158: The Great Secession & The Four Spheres**  
  Enraged by imperial taxes and the Ares disaster, the L-5 Lagrange orbital cylinders organized the **Outer Colony League (OCL)** for mutual defense. In the Jovian Trojans, deep-space pioneers formed the **Sidereal Sovereign League (Astrans)**, isolating themselves from Earth and adapting physiologically to low-gravity (*Homo Sideralis*). In the Asteroid Belt, corsair reavers and scrap refiners united under Madame Rosalind Cruz to codify the **Rim-Syndicate Cartel** at Scrap Forge 'Rust-Pike'.

* **Y159 – Y175: The Terran Coalition & The Baikal Complex**  
  Bankrupted by colonial blockades, the Old Hegemony collapsed into the militaristic **Terran Coalition (TC)**. Coalition High Command centralized its war industry inside the impenetrable granite bunkers of **Lake Baikal** on Earth. Meanwhile, Sato Heavy Industries expanded into **Apex Bio-Cybernetics Zaibatsu (ABC)**, establishing extraterritorial orbital redoubts and selling combat wetware and neural coprocessors to all warring factions.

---

### 2.4 Act IV: The Conventional Deadlock & The Bipedal Dead-Ends (Y176 – Y199)

* **Y176 – Y188: The Attritional Tank Wars**  
  Border clashes erupted across lunar plateaus and orbital transit routes, waged entirely with conventional heavy weapons: *T-720 Mammoth* 110-ton Main Battle Tanks, *A-14 Skyhammer* strike jets, and rail artillery crawlers. The war ground to a bloody stalemate: tracked tanks could not climb shattered lunar crags, breached orbital colonies, or vertical debris rubble, while strike jets lacked the armor and loiter time to hold territory.

* **Y189 – Y199: The Failure of the "Walking Gun"**  
  Terran engineers built early hydraulic bipeds (the *TC-X01 Golem*): clumsy 14-meter walkers powered by diesel turbines. They were catastrophic failures: heavy cast-tungsten plating sheared hydraulic seals, internal combustion engines exploded when hit, and they lacked compact reactor power. In Y198, Terran General Deborah Vance officially canceled all bipedal programs, declaring humanoid warframes an "unworkable engineering myth."

---

### 2.5 Act V: The Boiling Point & The Prometheus Catalyst (Y200 – Y205)

* **Y201 – Y203: The Tritium Choke & The Cascadia Massacre**  
  Colony union leader Joshua Reed organized a complete embargo of Jovian Helium-3 and Tritium to Earth, triggering continent-wide blackouts. In Y203, a Terran naval battlegroup clashed with Colony blockade forces in the Cascadia sector. The catastrophic engagement pulverized dozens of capital hulls, generating the radioactive, navigational hazard zone known as the **Cascadia Scrapline**.

* **Y204: The Discovery in the Rust (The Player's Origin)**  
  A destitute freelance space salvager (the Player), picking through irradiated wrecks in the Cascadia Belt, detected an anomalous electromagnetic signature inside an asteroid hollow. Breaching the rock, they discovered the sealed, cryo-quarantined hull of **Vessel 07 (APV Hephaestus / USN-X Hephaestus)**. Upon accessing the bridge, ancient Martian algorithms recognized the salvage beacon, re-igniting the **Automated Molecular Crucible Matrix**. Lost pre-war blueprints for compact Tokamak reactors, myomer artificial muscles, and molecular sintering were unlocked.

* **Early Y205 (Campaign Launch): The Catalyst Sortie**  
  Joshua Reed reads the **L-5 Covenant**, formally seceding from Earth. General Arthur Bradley responds with the **Baikal Directive**, launching a full-scale conventional invasion fleet. But aboard the *Hephaestus*, the player completes and sorties **Unit-01: The Ironclad Prototype**.  
  When an 18-meter bipedal machine leaps over trenches, shrugs off 120mm tank shells, and crushes armored turrets in close combat, 100 years of conventional doctrine is obliterated. The superpowers freeze hostilities to scramble for the technology, igniting the 365-day reverse-engineering arms race!

* **`datafiles/factions_lore.json`**: Matches `Phase 1: The Baikal Shock & The L-5 Secession (Y205 - Y208)` and establishes the player's role as commander of the sovereign dreadnought *Hephaestus*.
* **`datafiles/mobile_suits_timeline.json`**: Explains the pre-Y205 failed hydraulic prototypes (like the *TC-X01 Golem*) and grounds the subsequent 10-year tech explosion (Y205–Y215).
* **Character Dialogue Backstories (Docs 51–56)**:
  * **General Arthur Bradley (TC, 56):** Hardened by the Y203 Cascadia fuel riots and trench wars; believes in raw Baikal steel.
  * **Commander Joshua Reed (OCL, 44):** Bearing the generational scars and oxygen taxes dating back to the Copernicus Decrees.
  * **Captain Sonya Cruz (RSC, 39):** Born in the Asteroid Belt; daughter of the Scrapline clans that broke away in Y160.
  * **Director Kenjiro Sato (ABC, 48):** Carrying forward 80 years of Sato family transhumanist neural patents.
  * **Archon Orin Sideris (Astran, 62):** A product of 60 years of biological divergence since the Y155 Sidereal isolation.
  * **Consensus Node 0-Prime (Martian):** Continuously calculating entropy for 61 years since the Winter of Y144 in Olympus Mons.
