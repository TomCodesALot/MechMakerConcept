# 24. Dreadnought Vast Upgrade Tree, Division Refits & Endgame Progression System

## 1. Executive Vision: The Flagship as the Beating Heart of the PMC

In *MechMakerConcept*, the player's flagship—the pre-war experimental dreadnought **USN-X Hephaestus**—is not a passive background menu. It is the central engine of the player's entire private military empire:
- It is your **factory** (housing the only autonomous molecular nanofabricator in the Sol System).
- It is your **home and sanctuary** (housing your pilots, medical bays, and neuro-simulators).
- It is your **strategic mobility** (navigating between Earth, Mars, the Asteroid Belt, Jupiter, Saturn, and the Void).
- It is your **orbital artillery platform** (providing devastating kinetic and tachyon fire support in tactical combat).
- It is your **economic engine** (refining ores, deploying mining drone tenders, and siphoning corporate market share).

As the player progresses from a penniless Scrapline debris scavenger to a multi-planetary sovereign PMC hegemon, the *Hephaestus* evolves across **8 Ship Divisions** and **5 Progressive Tiers (40 Comprehensive Upgrades)** from patchwork scrap to terrifying pre-war archeotech supremacy.

## 2. The 8 Ship Divisions & 5-Tier Upgrade Progression

### Division 1: Molecular Fabrication Core (`fabrication_tier`)
*Controls chassis printing throughput, build bays, print speed, and S-grade prototype chance.*
* **Tier 1 (Scrapline Assembler)**: 1 Assembly Bay. Standard print duration.
* **Tier 2 (Dual Molecular Sintering Bays)**: 2 Assembly Bays. $-25\%$ build duration.  
  *Cost: 250,000 Cr | 5x Vanguard Composite*
* **Tier 3 (Tri-Core Nanite Crucible Matrix)**: 3 Assembly Bays. $-50\%$ build duration. $+5\%$ Masterwork S-Grade chance.  
  *Cost: 600,000 Cr | 5x Aerosteel Vernier Flux*
* **Tier 4 (Pre-War Archeotech Replicator)**: 4 Assembly Bays. $-70\%$ build duration. $+15\%$ S-Grade Masterwork chance.  
  *Cost: 1,500,000 Cr | 5x Adamantine Heavy Carbide*
* **Tier 5 (Omega Sovereign Matter-Synthesizer)**: 5 Assembly Bays. Instant alloy crucible blending. $+25\%$ S-Grade Masterwork chance. All player-forged mobile suits permanently gain $+15\%$ Base Armor & Power Output!  
  *Cost: 3,500,000 Cr | 5x Reactive Damascus*

---

### Division 2: Automated Mining Sills & Drone Refinery (`mining_tier`)
*Controls raw mineral harvesting, refining speed, and in-sphere planetary yield multipliers.*
* **Tier 1 (Manual Cargo Shuttles)**: $+25\%$ local mining node yield when flagship is stationed in sphere.
* **Tier 2 (Automated Magnetic Harvester Drones)**: $+50\%$ local mining node yield. Local storage capacity $+50\%$.  
  *Cost: 200,000 Cr | 5x Vanguard Composite*
* **Tier 3 (Deep-Core Plasma Tractor Beams)**: $+100\%$ local mining node yield. $+10\%$ bonus yield in rare isotopes (Tritium/Flux).  
  *Cost: 500,000 Cr | 5x Hyperion Superconductor Matrix*
* **Tier 4 (Molecular Nanite Centrifuge Sieve)**: $+150\%$ local mining node yield. Automatically purifies slag byproduct into usable Tungsten.  
  *Cost: 1,200,000 Cr | 5x Adamantine Heavy Carbide*
* **Tier 5 (Planetary Siphon Harvester Array)**: $+250\%$ local mining node yield! Passively generates $+15	ext{kg}$ raw elements per turn directly into Fleet Cargo even without an assigned mobile suit!  
  *Cost: 3,000,000 Cr | 5x Chobham-X Reactive Composite*

* **Tier 5 (Aegis Sovereign Fortress Barrier)**: Hull: $40,000	ext{ HP}$. Absolute immunity to all pirate node raids across the entire Sol System. Stationed flagship reflects $100\%$ incoming orbital assault damage!  
  *Cost: 2,800,000 Cr | 5x Chobham-X Reactive Composite*

### Division 4: Spinal Weaponry & Tactical Fire Support (`weapons_tier`)
*Unlocks and enhances player-commanded orbital kinetic and particle bombardment in tactical combat (`rm_combat`).*
* **Tier 1 (Point-Defense Autocannons)**: No tactical orbital strike available.
* **Tier 2 (Twin 600mm Spinal Mass Drivers)**: Unlocks `[CALL FLAGSHIP ORBITAL STRIKE]`. Deals $600$–$1,100$ kinetic damage across enemy grid (3-turn cooldown).  
  *Cost: 350,000 Cr | 5x Vanguard Composite*
* **Tier 3 (Heavy Spinal Particle Lance)**: Deals $1,200$–$1,800$ beam damage across enemy grid, melting trench cover (2-turn cooldown).  
  *Cost: 800,000 Cr | 5x Aerosteel Vernier Flux*
* **Tier 4 (Kinetic Rod Penetrator Array)**: Calls down Mach-12 orbital tungsten rods dealing $2,000$–$3,000$ catastrophic armor-crushing damage (2-turn cooldown).  
  *Cost: 2,000,000 Cr | 5x Adamantine Heavy Carbide*
* **Tier 5 (Doomsday Tachyon Orbital Cleaver)**: Fires an apocalyptic particle beam from orbit dealing $3,500$–$5,000$ damage (1-turn cooldown), instantly obliterating enemy frontline formations!  
  *Cost: 4,500,000 Cr | 5x Reactive Damascus*

## 3. Flagship Specialization Protocols (Master Doctrines)

Once the player reaches **Tier 3 in at least 4 Divisions**, the Command Bridge unlocks the ability to ratify one of **Three Mutually Exclusive Flagship Protocols**:

1. **Protocol A: The Industrial Forge Titan (Mass-Production Supremacy)**
   * Reduces all mobile suit component fabrication costs by $-40\%$.
   * Unlocks a permanent 6th Assembly Bay.
   * Stationed planetary mining node yields tripled ($3.0	imes$).
2. **Protocol B: The Orbital Siege Fortress (Military Dominance)**
   * Orbital Fire Support strikes deal $+50\%$ damage and pierce all structural armor.
   * Flagship hull gains an additional $+25,000	ext{ HP}$.
   * Deployed allied combat units gain $+35\%$ Kinetic Armor and $+15$ Heat Dissipation.
3. **Protocol C: The Shadow Panopticon (Espionage & Syndicate Monopoly)**
   * Instantaneous 0-Turn Tachyon Fold transit across Sol.
   * Rival corporate sabotage attempts are converted into $+50,000	ext{ Cr}$ black-market bounties.
   * Siphons $+2\%$ market share from all rival corporations every 4 turns.
