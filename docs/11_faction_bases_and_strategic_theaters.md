## 2. Comprehensive Faction Strategic Bases (Earth & Space)

### Faction: The Terran Coalition (TERRAN)
*   **Alignment:** United Earth Defense Assembly | **Doctrinal Alignment:** Heavy attritional siege warfare, entrenched defensive lines, 105mm-203mm solid-slug cannons, hydraulic shock absorbers capable of surviving planetary orbital drops.

#### [EARTH] Fortress Baikal — Supreme Continental Foundry
*   **Location / Coordinates:** `Southern Siberia, Lake Baikal Basin, Earth`
*   **Commanding Officer:** **General Deborah Vance**
*   **Strategic Importance:** Primary planetary manufacturing center for Vanguard Defense Systems. Subterranean foundries extending 2,200m beneath permafrost.
*   **Defensive Fortifications & Garrison:** Concentric triple trench line, TCM-03 Bulwark static bunkers, GHA-07G mud platoons, automated 155mm coastal rail-howitzers.
*   **Primary Manufacturing & Outputs:** GHA-07 Ironclad [Line Type], GHA-07G Trench Type, GHA-07B Siege Breaker, Tungsten-Carbide Slab Armor

#### [EARTH] Mount Kenya Sky-Anchor 'Ascent-1'
*   **Location / Coordinates:** `Equatorial East African Ridge, Earth`
*   **Commanding Officer:** **High Commissioner Arthur Sterling**
*   **Strategic Importance:** Terrestrial anchor of destroyed space elevator, converted into heavy-lift electromagnetic mass-driver launch gantry.
*   **Defensive Fortifications & Garrison:** Multi-tier concrete bastions, GHA-08 Flak Colossus air-defense regiments, stratified SAM silos.
*   **Primary Manufacturing & Outputs:** Trans-Atmospheric Dropships, Orbital Drop Pods, Heavy Kinetic Munitions

#### [SPACE] Luna Fortress 'Archimedes Crater'
*   **Location / Coordinates:** `Archimedes Crater, Mare Imbrium, Earth's Moon`
*   **Commanding Officer:** **Supreme Field Marshal Gregory Holt**
*   **Strategic Importance:** Terran High Command's primary lunar bastion, naval dreadnought drydocks, and Senate Defense Directorate.
*   **Defensive Fortifications & Garrison:** TCX-11 Behemoth Mk.IV surface-to-orbit railguns, overlapping PDC laser grids, lunar maglev transit.
*   **Primary Manufacturing & Outputs:** TCX-11 Behemoth Platforms, Heavy Lunar Siege Modules, Hyperion D-T Tokamaks

#### [SPACE] Citadel Gibraltar
*   **Location / Coordinates:** `Earth-Moon L1 Lagrangian Transit Corridor`
*   **Commanding Officer:** **Commodore Valerie Thorne**
*   **Strategic Importance:** Massive military staging fortress wheel and orbital checkpoint inspecting all freight arriving from outer colonies.
*   **Defensive Fortifications & Garrison:** GHA-07S Space Patrol wings, twin naval dorsal railguns, anti-torpedo mesh nets.
*   **Primary Manufacturing & Outputs:** GHA-07S Space Patrol Variants, Orbital Reaction Fuel Cells

### Faction: The Outer Colony League (COLONIES)
*   **Alignment:** Jovian-Larange Liberation Compact | **Doctrinal Alignment:** Zero-G vacuum skirmishing, hit-and-run particle beam strikes, electronic warfare jamming (ECM), agile thrust-to-weight ratios bypassing heavy artillery arcs.

#### [SPACE] Ganymede Orbital Nexus 'Olympus Commune'
*   **Location / Coordinates:** `Low Ganymede Orbit, Jovian System`
*   **Commanding Officer:** **Minister Lyra Thorne**
*   **Strategic Importance:** Capital and industrial soul of the League. Interconnected bio-domes and zero-G automated electron-beam sintering shipyards harvesting Helium-3.
*   **Defensive Fortifications & Garrison:** Autonomous drone sensor perimeters, revolving launch bays with elite OCM-09 Aether Stryker wings, particle beam batteries.
*   **Primary Manufacturing & Outputs:** OCM-09 Aether Stryker Standard, Helios-9 Tokamaks, 3D Sintered Cermet Honeycombs

#### [SPACE] Fortress Valhalla
*   **Location / Coordinates:** `L4 Trojan Asteroid Swarm, Jupiter-Sun Orbit`
*   **Commanding Officer:** **Fleet Commander Cassian Vane**
*   **Strategic Importance:** Covert military headquarters of the Liberation Flotilla. Carved inside a 40km nickel-iron asteroid with flush-mounted hangar doors.
*   **Defensive Fortifications & Garrison:** Sorren Lin's Starlight Phantom squadron, Kira Dahl's ECM radar decoys, concealed spinal beam cannons.
*   **Primary Manufacturing & Outputs:** OCA-07 Helios Arbalest Cannons, OCM-09S/GT Starlight Phantoms, Swept Vernier Binders

#### [SPACE] L5 Solar Scrubber Array 'Dawn-7'
*   **Location / Coordinates:** `Earth-Moon L5 Lagrangian Point`
*   **Commanding Officer:** **Chief Engineer Tarek Al-Mansoor**
*   **Strategic Importance:** Multi-kilometer photovoltaic mirrors and oxygen scrubbers supplying life support to millions of orbital habitat citizens.
*   **Defensive Fortifications & Garrison:** Automated defense drone buoys, OCM-09C Flight Leader squadrons, laser-scattering chaff pods.
*   **Primary Manufacturing & Outputs:** Synthetic Flux, Tritium Fuel Refining, Breathable O2 Reserves

#### [EARTH] Red Sands Deep Sanctuary
*   **Location / Coordinates:** `Atacama High Plateau, South America, Earth`
*   **Commanding Officer:** **Captain Nadia 'Mirage' Sorel**
*   **Strategic Importance:** Covert planetary forward base concealed beneath an abandoned lithium borehole. Staging ground for OCM-09G atmospheric guerrilla strikes.
*   **Defensive Fortifications & Garrison:** Optical thermal camouflage tarps, subterranean blast lifts, mobile seismic decoy jammers.
*   **Primary Manufacturing & Outputs:** OCM-09G Strike Lander Refits, Hybrid Beam/Kinetic Carbines

## 3. Gameplay & Strategic Mechanics Integration

1.  **Contract Operations (docs/04):**
    *   Factions issue high-paying **Base Defense Sorties** when their forward bases come under siege.
    *   Contracting with General Vance to defend Fortress Baikal yields massive Credit bonuses and access to heavy GHA-07 blueprints, but instantly damages standing with the Outer Colony League.
    *   Infiltrating or raiding bases (e.g. assaulting The Slag Heap on Ceres or raiding the Atacama Quantum Vault) allows the player's PMC to loot classified prototypes and rare alloy caches.
2.  **Resource Logistics (docs/06):**
    *   Mining nodes on the War Map (Tab 3 Subtab 0) route their supply freighters through these strategic choke points:
        *   Freight passing through **Station Gibraltar** is subject to Terran Coalition tariff searches unless escorted by player mobile suits.
        *   Freight navigating near **Tortuga-Void** suffers high pirate ambush threat from Torval's reavers unless the player possesses positive standing with Madame Cruz.
3.  **In-Engine UI Expansion (obj_HQTerminal):**
    *   Command Deck Tab 3 Subtab 1 (Faction Dossiers) and Subtab 0 (Strategic War Map) reflect these major bases with active garrison readouts, regional threat meters, and liaison briefing channels.
